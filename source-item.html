Product sourcing

Cannot find it? Send the part number.

An unsuccessful catalogue search should not end the sale. Share the SKU, manufacturer part number, brand or a photograph and ask Tillman Tough to check availability.

Turn missing products into opportunities.

This route is especially useful when a customer has:

  • An exact manufacturer part number
  • A discontinued or replacement item
  • A specification but no product name
  • A large quantity requirement
  • An item that has not been added to the online catalogue
Inside Wix, this would create a tagged sourcing lead and alert the appropriate sales contact.
